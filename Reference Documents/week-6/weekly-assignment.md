##### Part 1:

Shop receipt: students must print the whole receipt with a single print(), with no typed spaces for alignment and a C:\\Shop\\bill.txt path that needs \\\\.

Bug hunt: now 7 bugs instead of 5. The hardest is age \* 12: it doesn't crash, it just repeats "20" twelve times, so students have to spot that the output is wrong.



##### Part 2: 

three programs that have to cope with messy input:

Email maker: builds priya.sharma@acmelabs.com and initials from badly typed names.

Bill splitter: tip and per-person share, shown to 2 decimal places.

Life in numbers: a tab-aligned table of their age in months, weeks, days and hours.



##### Part 3: 

a secret message encoder. Students clean the text, swap letters for numbers, reverse it and draw a \* box sized to fit any message. A bonus task asks them to write the decoder.
