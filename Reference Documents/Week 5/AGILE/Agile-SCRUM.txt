SCRUM – QUICK BYTES

Scrum

* Scrum is an Agile framework used to develop software incrementally.
* Work is divided into short, fixed periods called Sprints.
* A Sprint typically lasts 1–4 weeks.
* Each Sprint aims to produce a potentially usable product increment.

Scrum Roles

* Product Owner – manages priorities and Product Backlog.
* Scrum Master – facilitates Scrum and helps remove impediments.
* Developers – build and deliver the product increment.

Scrum Artifacts

* Product Backlog – complete list of desired work.
* Sprint Backlog – work selected for the current Sprint.
* Increment – completed, usable output of the Sprint.

Scrum Events

1. Sprint Planning – decide what to build.
2. Daily Scrum – short daily team synchronization.
3. Sprint Review – demonstrate the completed work.
4. Sprint Retrospective – discuss how to improve.
5. Sprint – the overall development cycle.

Simple Flow

Product Backlog → Sprint Planning → Sprint → Daily Scrum → Increment → Review → Retrospective → Next Sprint

In one line:

Scrum = Agile framework + short Sprints + continuous feedback + working software.
