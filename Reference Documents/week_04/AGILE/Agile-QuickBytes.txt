AGILE – QUICK BYTES

Agile

* Agile is an iterative and flexible approach to software development.
* Work is delivered in small, incremental releases.
* It focuses on customer collaboration, continuous feedback, and adapting to change.
* Development and testing happen continuously throughout the project.
* Agile encourages close collaboration between cross-functional teams.

Core Values

* Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.
* Working software over comprehensive documentation.
* Customer collaboration over contract negotiation.
* Responding to change over following a plan.

Key Characteristics

* Iterative and incremental development.
* Frequent customer feedback.
* Continuous testing and integration.
* Flexible response to changing requirements.
* Continuous improvement.
* Strong team collaboration.

Common Agile Frameworks

* Scrum – development through timeboxed Sprints.
* Kanban – continuous workflow using visual boards.
* SAFe – scaling Agile across large organizations.

Typical Agile Flow

Requirements → Planning → Development → Testing → Review → Feedback → Improvement → Next Iteration

Advantages

* Faster delivery of working software.
* Easy adaptation to changing requirements.
* Early identification of issues.
* Continuous customer involvement.
* Frequent delivery of business value.

Challenges

* Requires active stakeholder involvement.
* Changing requirements can affect scope.
* Requires strong team collaboration and discipline.
* Less predictable when requirements change frequently.

In one line:

Agile = Iterative development + Customer collaboration + Continuous feedback + Adaptability to change.
