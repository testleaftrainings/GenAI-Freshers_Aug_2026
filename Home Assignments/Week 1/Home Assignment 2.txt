*Group Assignment: AI Models and AGI Benchmarks*

Form a group of **three members** and choose any one AI model, such as a GPT model, Gemini, Claude, Llama, or DeepSeek.

Prepare a presentation covering:

* Introduction to the selected AI model
* Meaning and purpose of AGI benchmarks
* Benchmarks used to evaluate the model
* Capabilities tested by each benchmark
* How the model is evaluated and scored
* Performance of the selected model
* Comparison with other leading models
* Strengths and limitations of benchmark results
* Whether benchmark performance reflects real-world capability
* Your group’s final observations

**Important:** Benchmarks are primarily used to evaluate trained models; models are not usually “trained in a benchmark.” Use reliable sources and mention the source of every benchmark result included in your presentation.

All three members must participate in preparing and delivering the presentation.
