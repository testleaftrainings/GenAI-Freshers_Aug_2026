*Home Assignment: Machine Learning Use-Case Presentation*

Choose any real-world Machine Learning use case that is either:

* Currently implemented and in use, or
* Still in the research and development stage

Prepare a presentation explaining:

* The selected domain and its problem
* How Machine Learning is applied
* The type of data used
* The benefits and business impact
* Challenges and limitations
* Future improvements and possibilities
* A real-world example, if available

Be prepared to present and explain your chosen use case clearly during the session.
