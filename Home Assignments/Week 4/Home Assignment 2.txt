*Home Assignment: Architecture and MCQ Creation*

For your assigned domain:

* Create a clear end-to-end architecture diagram for the proposed application.
* Show all major components, data flow, technologies, integrations, inputs, and outputs.
* Prepare **20 multiple-choice questions** related to the assigned domain and architecture.
* Provide four options for each question.
* Clearly mention the correct answer.
* Include questions of easy, medium, and difficult levels.
* Avoid repeating the same concepts across multiple questions.

Be prepared to present and explain both the architecture and MCQs during the session.
