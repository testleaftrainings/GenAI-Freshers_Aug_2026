*Home Assignment: Prompt-Writing Techniques Presentation*

Explore prompt-writing techniques similar to the **ICEPOT framework** and select any one technique.

Prepare a presentation explaining:

* The name and structure of the technique
* The purpose of each component
* How the technique improves an LLM’s response
* The industries or domains where it can be applied

* How the prompting technique helps solve that problem
* A comparison of the output before and after applying the technique
* Benefits, limitations, and best practices
